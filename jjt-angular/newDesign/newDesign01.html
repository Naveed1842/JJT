Daniel's story

Daniel is in Grade 5 at Riverside Primary in Kisumu. He loves mathematics and dreams of becoming an engineer. He walks 40 minutes to school each day and rarely misses a class.

His family works seasonal farm labour, which makes school fees unpredictable. Your sponsorship covers tuition, books, uniform, and a daily meal — and the Continuity Fund ensures none of it lapses between payments.
