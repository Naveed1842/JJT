Bilal's story

Bilal is in Grade 5 at a government primary school in Lahore. He loves mathematics and rarely misses a class, walking 30 minutes each way.

His father is a daily-wage labourer, which makes school costs unpredictable. Your sponsorship covers tuition, books, uniform, and a daily meal — and the Continuity Fund ensures none of it lapses between payments.

How are children selected?
By financial need, identified with partner schools and verified in person by our local field team before any profile goes live.
How is Zakat handled?
Zakat gifts are kept separate and spent only on eligible children, under the guidance of qualified scholars.
Can I see progress?
Yes — sponsors receive photo updates, term reports, and attendance for their child, twice a year.
Who governs JJT?
A registered board with published accounts and an external annual audit. Registration number shown in the footer.

Why education is a noble cause
"The likeness of those who spend their wealth in the way of Allah is as the likeness of a grain that grows seven ears, in every ear a hundred grains."
Surah Al-Baqarah, 2:261 · translation to be verified before launch

Educating a child carries three rewards at once

References below are well-known and authentic; exact wording will be confirmed against an approved translation and reviewed by a qualified scholar before publishing.

01 · ONGOING CHARITY
Sadaqah Jariyah
Among the deeds whose reward continues after death are ongoing charity and beneficial knowledge. — Sahih Muslim
02 · CARING FOR ORPHANS
Standing beside the orphan
The Prophet ﷺ described the closeness in Paradise of the one who cares for an orphan. — Sahih al-Bukhari
03 · GIVING DOES NOT DIMINISH
Wealth is not decreased by charity
It is reported that charity does not decrease wealth. — Sahih Muslim

Even one rupee has value

Not everyone can sponsor a whole child — and you don't have to. Every contribution, however small, joins the central fund and helps keep a child learning. No amount is small in reward.
