Bilal's story

Bilal is ten, in Grade 5, and he loves mathematics. Every morning he walks half an hour to his school in Lahore — and he almost never misses a day.

His father earns day to day, so school fees do not always come easy. Every month, you give Bilal Bilal's fees, books, uniform and a warm meal each school day — and the Continuity Fund makes sure his learning never stops, even if a payment is late.

How Bilal was chosen: by need, not by chance. Our team sat with his family and verified everything in person. See our selection process →

How are children selected?
We look for the families who need help most. Partner schools point us to them, and our own team visits each home before a child ever appears on this site.
How is Zakat handled?
Zakat is kept in its own account and spent only on children who qualify, with the guidance of qualified scholars.
Can I see progress?
Yes. Twice a year you receive photos, report cards and attendance for your child.
Who governs JJT?
A registered board, published accounts, and an outside audit every year. Our registration number sits at the bottom of every page.

Why educate a child?
"The likeness of those who spend their wealth in the way of Allah is as the likeness of a grain that grows seven ears, in every ear a hundred grains."
Surah Al-Baqarah, 2:261 · translation to be verified before launch

One gift. Three rewards.

References below are well-known and authentic; exact wording will be confirmed against an approved translation and reviewed by a qualified scholar before publishing.

01 · ONGOING CHARITY
Sadaqah Jariyah
When a person dies, their deeds end — except a few. Ongoing charity and beneficial knowledge are two of them. Education is both. — Sahih Muslim
02 · CARING FOR ORPHANS
Standing beside the orphan
The Prophet ﷺ said the one who cares for an orphan will be close to him in Paradise — like two fingers held together. — Sahih al-Bukhari
03 · GIVING DOES NOT DIMINISH
Wealth is not decreased by charity
Giving does not make you poorer. The Prophet ﷺ taught that charity never decreases wealth. — Sahih Muslim

No gift is too small

Not everyone can sponsor a whole child — and that is okay. Your Rs 100 joins someone else's Rs 100, and together a child stays in school. In the sight of Allah, no sincere gift is small.
