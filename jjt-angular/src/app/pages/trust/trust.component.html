How are children selected?
By financial need, identified with partner schools and verified in person by our local field team before any profile goes live.
How is Zakat handled?
Zakat gifts are kept separate and spent only on eligible children, under the guidance of qualified scholars. Every rupee of Zakat is tracked independently.
Can I see progress?
Yes — sponsors receive photo updates, term reports, and attendance for their child, twice a year. You'll know exactly how your child is doing.
Who governs JJT?
A registered board with published accounts and an external annual audit. Registered under the Trust Act 1882. Registration number shown in the footer.
The Continuity Fund

A child's education never stops because of a gap in giving.

A small portion of every sponsorship contribution funds the central Continuity Fund. If a sponsor ever pauses or a payment is delayed, the fund ensures the child's fees, meals, and supplies continue without interruption.

You can also give directly to the fund if you want to contribute without committing to a specific child.

Zero days missed
Every child remains in school even when a sponsorship lapses.
Transparent bridging
When a child is covered, their profile shows "Covered by the fund" — visible to any visitor.
Minimum reserve kept
A protected reserve is always maintained to cover at least one full term.
