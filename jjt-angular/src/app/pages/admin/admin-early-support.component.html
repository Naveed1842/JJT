What is Early Support?

Early support is financial aid provided to children before an official sponsorship relationship is established. This helps us track humanitarian assistance and build relationships with potential sponsors.

  • Pre-sponsorship financial aid
  • Emergency support funds
  • Initial onboarding assistance

Best Practices

  • Always verify child ID before recording
  • Use consistent currency codes
  • Record support promptly after provision
  • Maintain accurate financial records
  • Document with proper authorization
