Platform Flow — 6 Steps
1
Register Child
Add a child with roll number, campus, and monthly education cost. A ledger is created automatically.
2
Record Early Support
While seeking a sponsor, record months JJT funded directly. This creates ledger entries and allows progress updates.
3
Register Sponsor
Add a sponsor record. Copy the Sponsor ID — it is needed to create their login account.
4
Commit Sponsorship
Link the sponsor to a child from a future start month. Status is PENDING until payment is verified.
5
Activate Sponsorship
Once payment is received, activate the sponsorship from the Commitments view. Status becomes ACTIVE.
6
Add Monthly Progress
Each month, add a progress update for any child with a ledger entry. Sponsors see this in their portal.
Child Status Reference
Seeking No sponsor. Eligible for early support from JJT fund.
Reserved Sponsor committed, payment pending. Cannot accept another commitment.
Sponsored Active sponsorship. Sponsor pays monthly. Progress visible in portal.
Key Rules
One active or pending sponsorship per child at any time.
One ledger entry per month per child. Duplicates are blocked.
Progress can only be added for months that have a ledger entry.
Sponsorship start month must be in the future (next month or later).
